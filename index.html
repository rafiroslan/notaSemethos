IBADAH · UNIT 1 Diriku Bersih: Istinjak

Istinjak ialah membersihkan najis yang keluar dari qubul atau dubur.

💧 Alat beristinjak

Air mutlak (paling afdal) dan benda kesat: batu, kertas, kertas tisu, daun kering, kulit kayu.

🪨 Ciri benda kesat

  • Kesat (batu, kertas)
  • Bukan benda dimuliakan (makanan)
  • Bersih dan suci
  • Boleh menghilangkan najis (bukan licin seperti plastik, kaca)

✅ Syarat istinjak selain air

  • Najis tidak beralih dari tempat asal
  • Najis masih belum kering

🧻 Cara istinjak selain air

  • Sapu dengan benda kesat
  • Sapu 3 kali
  • Setiap sapuan guna bahagian yang berbeza

UNIT 6 Length, Mass & Volume

UnitsRemember
📏 Lengthcm, m1 m = 100 cm
⚖️ Massg, kg1 kg = 1000 g
🧃 Volume of liquidmℓ, ℓ1 ℓ = 1000 mℓ

Short or light things → cm, g, mℓ (pencil, eraser, a spoon of medicine). Long or heavy things → m, kg, ℓ (classroom, watermelon, a bucket of water).

Measure from the 0 mark on the ruler. Read liquid at eye level. A big object is not always heavier: mass does not depend on size.

UNIT 6 Sources of Light

☀️ The Sun

The main source of light during the day. It is a natural source of light.

💡 Lamps

Street lamps, car lamps and lamps at home.

🔥 Fire

Candle flame, oil lamp, torch.

In a lighted area we can do activities easily. In a dark area we need light to do activities.

UNIT 6 Shadows & Clarity of Shadows

A shadow is formed when light is blocked by an object.

The clarity of a shadow depends on the object. In a shadow play (wayang kulit) the shadow is clear because the puppet does not allow light to pass through.

UNIT 9 Water

Natural sources: 🌧️ rain · 🏞️ river · 🌊 sea · lake · waterfall · spring.

Water flows from a high place to a low place.

Sun heats water → water vapour rises (evaporation) → cools into clouds → falls as rain → flows back to rivers and sea.

UNIT 9 Air

Air is all around us. It has no colour and we cannot see it, but we can feel it.

Moving air = wind 🌬️

  • 👕 dries clothes
  • 🪁 flies a kite
  • ⛵ moves a sailboat
  • 🌀 turns a windmill

Very strong wind (storm) can break trees and roofs.

Wind rocket: blow air into a straw or balloon, and the moving air pushes the rocket forward.

  • 🔋 Rehat memulihkan tenaga

5 Kesan Tembakau, Alkohol & Dadah

🚬 Tembakau (rokok)

Batuk, sakit paru-paru, gigi kuning, mulut berbau.

🍺 Alkohol

Mabuk, merosakkan hati. Haram bagi orang Islam.

💊 Dadah

Ketagihan, khayal, merosakkan otak.

Katakan TIDAK! ✋ Beritahu ibu bapa atau guru jika ditawarkan.

TEMPO & IRAMA Tempo Cepat, Lambat & Tepuk Irama

Tempo = kelajuan muzik.

🐇 Cepat

Arnab melompat, kuda berlari. Lagu gembira.

🐢 Lambat

Kura-kura, siput berjalan. Lagu tenang.

Tepuk irama: tepuk mengikut ketukan lagu.
ta = 1 tepuk · ti-ti = 2 tepuk cepat dalam 1 ketuk · sh = senyap (rehat)

Pilih corak dan tempo, tekan Main, kemudian tepuk bersama!
